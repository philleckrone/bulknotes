Bulk Note Editing is a free Paratext 9 plugin that lists the notes of one or more projects and resolves, assigns or marks as read many of them at once. Choose a verse, a chapter, a book or the whole project, then narrow the list by status, read or unread, author, assigned person, date, words or tag (including conflict notes). Tick the notes you want, then resolve them, with an optional comment, assign them to a team member, or mark them as read or unread. The tool never deletes a note: every change is a new comment, so each note's history stays complete.
